# Case Continuity — Scope

The participant proposed the name on 26 October. This edition translates and consolidates the approved scope. Historical progress/authorization notes remain in the Spanish original; the dated release record defines current status.

## Unique Kernel

Resume a case from reviewed, explainable changes without turning conditions into commitments, interest into contracting authorization, or attributed statements into verified facts. Show what changed, what remained, what is pending and the source of each incorporation. Quotations alone do not prove this; persisted effects must preserve original limits/context.

## Audience and Core Loop

A commercial adviser reviewing fiber and two mobile lines for a fictional customer. They need needs, budget, important conditions, offers, missing documents and decisions without rebuilding a conversation.

Open saved case → introduce one dated/attributed update → compare it with existing information → review individual proposals/citations → accept, edit or reject → explicitly save approved incorporations → explicitly finish review → close/reopen with sources and history.

Partial saving does not declare the whole update reviewed. Analysis, closing a screen and accepting cards do not complete review. A no-change review adds a dated note only after explicit confirmation, retaining state, next action and data version. A useful attributed statement may be incorporated as an approved antecedent. Repeating an unchanged review does not duplicate notes or empty versions.

## POC Boundary

One fictional case per trial, one update per review, one complete flow through recovery. Independent test cases can be created without deleting prior trials. Pasted notes/document text; no upload/OCR. Proposals preserve source, conditions and uncertainty. Only reviewed changes are incorporated; confirmed history and previous values remain accessible. Local Spanish interface with English materials/testing translations.

In-scope data can legitimately change: needs, fiber speed, mobile count, unconditional budget, conditional willingness, offers, actual document receipt, preferences, interest, antecedents, uncertainties, state and next action. Only contracting authorization is outside V1 writes; that restriction is not evidence of understanding.

Unresolved actions survive new waiting/follow-up. Remove them only with explicit resolution, cancellation or replacement backed by the source. Do not invent ordering, deadlines, priorities or performed work.

## Inspiration and Identity

Prior continuity/provenance/human-authority concepts informed requirements; no code or records were transferred from existing applications. A sober review screen, not a chat transcript or full CRM. The folder identifier was not a product name.

## Working Result

The principal example corrects declared fiber speed, keeps EUR40 as reference and willingness to consider EUR50 conditional on both lines and stable pricing for twelve months. Interest cannot become authorization. Saved values, prior facts, limits, citations and pending tasks survive reopening.

Six independent families check conditional budget, correction, document receipt, interest without authorization, conflicting conditions and no news. Positive variants require approved changes; read-only fields cannot manufacture success. [Evidence](evidence.md) separates current and historical results.

## Explicitly Cut

No OCR/PDF processing, email/WhatsApp/CRM integration, real tariff comparisons, contracting, real customer data, automatic commercial actions, AI-decided priorities, public hosting or multi-user features. No feature expansion for presentation. Publication requires review and explicit authorization.

## Continue or Stop

Interest converted to authorization, lost conditions, unapproved writes, false sources or review completion without confirmation are critical failures; other correct cases cannot compensate. Evaluate original interpretation, controls and recovery independently.

Time saving is unmeasured. Compare full waiting/reading/correction/saving effort with a manual update before claiming benefit. If correction costs as much without a provenance benefit, reconsider rather than polish. Six cases do not prove general reliability. The participant owns final readiness, naming and public statements.
